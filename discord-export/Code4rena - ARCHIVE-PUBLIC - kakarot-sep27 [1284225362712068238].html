fcremo 09/30/2024 15:19
The first is, I encourage you to look at the early parts of the transaction flow, specifically the entrypoint into the account contract. This is mainly because it was refactored after our audit.
15:20
In the commit we reviewed, account contracts natively supported the account abstraction __verify__ and __execute__ entrypoints; this is not the case anymore, and a bug in there could easily be a high severity issue (e.g. sig bypass or denial of service).
15:22
Another interesting idea is differential fuzzing the interpreter vs. geth (or another EVM implementation). It's something I would have really liked doing, but didn't get a chance to, as our audit prioritized a manual code review of the whole codebase. Despite Kakarot passes (a majority of) the official ethereum foundation compliance tests, we still found two (IIRC) opcode semantics bugs which were not covered by the testsuite (!). I'm convinced both would have been found by differential fuzzing. (edited)
15:26
I think achieving good performance could prove a challenging engineering effort, mostly because I'm not sure how efficient the Cairo0 code can be made to run. On the plus side, the executor component of the differential fuzzer could prove useful for hunting bugs in other Cairo0 (and perhaps even Cairo1) projects, so it could be a worthwile investment. Obligatory disclaimer: this is not financial time investment advice
